What is this?

Risk per trade is the money you accept to lose if the price reaches your stop-loss. Lotcheck turns it into a lot size. Use percent to risk a share of your balance each time, or a fixed amount if you prefer a set sum.

    What is this?

    The stop-loss decides how far the price may move against you. Lotcheck works out the size so that this move costs about your chosen risk. If the stop is below the entry it is a buy, if it is above the entry it is a sell. Spread, slippage and commission make the real loss bigger, so enter them if you know them.

    What is this?

    Some accounts have rules that end the account if you lose too much in a day or in total. Lotcheck works out how much room is left and sizes the trade so that the planned loss at your stop stays inside that room. Gaps and slippage can still make the loss larger. It is only as right as the numbers you enter.
